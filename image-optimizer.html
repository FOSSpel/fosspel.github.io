Frequently Asked Questions

What formats are supported?

All common image formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF. Input is handled via browser's Canvas API, output is WebP or MozJPEG.

Is there quality loss?

Yes, compression reduces file size by removing some image data. We let you control the quality/size tradeoff with the slider. Most users find 85% visually identical to original but 70-80% smaller.

How is this free? What's the catch?

No catch! {FOSSpel} is a faith-based project. We believe digital tools should serve people, not corporations. No ads, no tracking, no hidden fees — forever.

Do you remove EXIF metadata?

Yes, automatically. When you compress an image, all EXIF data (GPS, camera model, date, software) is stripped. Your privacy is our priority.

Can I optimize multiple images?

Currently, we process one image at a time for best quality control. But there's no limit — you can optimize hundreds of images, one after another.

Is this really private?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas. Your images are never uploaded to any server. You can even disconnect from the internet after loading the page!
